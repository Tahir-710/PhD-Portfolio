CASP–GCU LAHORE Jul 2021–Jun 2022

MgO thin films by pulsed laser deposition: repetition rate and film growth

Thesis

B.S. Thesis, UMT Lahore (2022), external placement at CASP, GCU

Supervisor: Hira Yaseen

Why this project. My first research project: how the laser repetition rate and total shot count in pulsed laser deposition set the thickness, crystallinity and grain morphology of MgO films on silicon.

What I did

Operated a Q-switched Nd:YAG PLD system (1064 nm, 200 mJ, 10 ns pulses, 10⁻³ Torr) to grow MgO films on silicon, varying the repetition rate across 2, 3, 4 and 5 Hz for 2000 to 5000 total shots.

Analyzed each film’s XRD (phase, crystallite size via Scherrer), SEM (surface morphology and cross-sectional thickness), and AFM (surface topography) data.

What I found

Film thickness rose from 307 nm (2000 shots) to 960 nm (5000 shots); crystallite size fell from 65 to 15 nm (mean 48 nm), and the MgO reflection changed from (220), seen up to 747 nm, to (311) alone in the thickest 960 nm film.
