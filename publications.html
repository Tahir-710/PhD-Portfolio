Publications

Peer-reviewed papers

Both published in 2025. In each I made the devices and measured them: fabrication, the optical test setup and the photocurrent measurements.

2025

Demonstrating donor–acceptor covalent conjugate of polythiophene and perylene diimide as an all-organic photocatalyst for enhanced light-driven H2O2 production

Iqbal, S., Akbar, F., Salar, M. A., Butt, T. S., Khan, A. A., Sohail, M., & Yameen, B.

ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces 17(19), 28609–28621 (2025)

My contributionFabricated the P3HT, P3HT-PDI-PH and PTh-PDI-DCP photodetectors; built the chopped-light optical setup; measured and analyzed the transient photocurrent, including rise and fall times. This work is Figure 3 of the paper.
10.1021/acsami.4c22745 ↗
2025

Temperature-dependent photoluminescence and grain size control in a prototypical lead-free bismuth halide perovskite

Jawad, M., Siddique, S., Aslam, Z., Hussain, S., Butt, T. S., Saeed, F., Ain, Q. U., Ulhaq, A., & Khan, A. A.

Advanced Engineering Materials 27(5), 2402009 (2025)

My contributionFabricated MA3Bi2I9 thin films with anti-solvent grain-size control, working with the lab team (Fig. 2, Table 1); fabricated the photodetectors and measured their photocurrent response (Fig. S2, Supporting Information). Author contributions listed in the paper: data curation, investigation and methodology (supporting).
